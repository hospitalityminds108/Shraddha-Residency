Welcome to Sharda Residency – The Best Hotel in Patna

Come and enjoy a comfortable and welcoming stay at Sharda Residency, the best hotel in Patna for both business and family trips. Located in a quiet area close to the city center, we offer cozy rooms, a large banquet hall, and delicious, home-style food at Cafe Sharda.

Our 21 modern rooms are designed for your comfort, with bright interiors and everything you need for a relaxing stay. Whether you’re here for work, a special event, or to explore Patna, we make sure your experience is smooth and memorable.

With personalized service, clean food, and thoughtfully designed spaces, Sharda Residency is where comfort meets convenience.
